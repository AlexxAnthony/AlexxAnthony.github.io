Comparing clustering algorithms

A co-authored research paper from my BSc at Sharda University. It compares K-means, DBSCAN and hierarchical clustering on 18,185 rice grain measurements, using PCA to reduce ten features to two and the silhouette score to judge the clusters. DBSCAN scored highest (0.57) and was the only method to flag outliers, picking out 23 unusual grains.

Python, scikit-learn, PCA, K-means, DBSCAN, hierarchical clustering
